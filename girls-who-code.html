Still Part of the Sisterhood

I didn't just pass through Girls Who Code once — I went through two of their programs, and both left their mark. The first cracked open my curiosity about what code could actually build; the second turned that curiosity into real skill, from web development to cybersecurity to data science, alongside a room full of girls asking the same nervous, excited questions I was.

What's stayed with me longest isn't a specific lesson, though — it's the sisterhood itself. Girls Who Code was built on the idea that closing the gender gap in tech happens one relationship at a time, not just one classroom at a time, and that's exactly how it felt: less like a course I completed and more like a community I joined for good. I'm still in touch with alumni from my cohorts, still cheering on the next group of scholars, and still proud to call myself part of it.
