Beyond the Metrics

Global Diplomacy

I'm endlessly curious about how people from completely different backgrounds find a way to talk to each other, negotiate, and actually move forward together.

AIM MUN will forever hold a really special place in my heart. The amazing executive board and learnings I gained were exceptional. From late nights fine-tuning conference logistics to the buzz of watching a committee session come together, I found a group of people who pushed me to think faster, listen harder, and lead with a lot more patience than I thought I had.

Serving alongside such a dedicated board taught me lessons in trust, follow-through, and quiet leadership that no classroom could have given me, and I still carry pieces of that experience into every room I walk into today.

That curiosity is what pulled me into Model United Nations in the first place, and it's what keeps me there as Undersecretary General of Internal Affairs for AIM Model UN. My role sits behind the scenes, training delegates on public speaking, negotiation, and diplomatic tact, while keeping the internal machinery of our conferences running smoothly.

What I love most is watching a delegate go from nervous and unsure to genuinely persuasive over the course of a single committee session. There's a real skill to disagreeing with someone while still keeping the conversation open, and helping people build that skill feels like some of the most useful work I do.

Diplomacy, to me, isn't just a debate format. It's a way of thinking about complex problems that carries over into how I approach data, business, and pretty much everything else I care about.

  • Delegate training Coaching public speaking, negotiation, and diplomatic strategy for MUN delegates.
  • Internal operations Keeping conference logistics and internal affairs running smoothly behind the scenes.
  • Cross-cultural dialogue A continued interest in how differing perspectives find common ground.
