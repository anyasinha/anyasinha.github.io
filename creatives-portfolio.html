Creatives Portfolio

Before I was a data person, I was a design person, and that visual instinct still shapes almost everything I make.

My creative work started with graphic design at AIM Explorer, where I helped shape rebranding campaigns and set the visual identity that our marketing community still uses today. Moving from that designer role into President of AIM Explorer meant trading the design software for team leadership, but the eye for what makes a story visually compelling never really left.

Since then, that instinct has shown up everywhere: in the social campaigns I built for Wadhwani Foundation's FH Moms initiative, in the promotional content I create as a Duolingo English Test Ambassador, and in how I present data itself, because a chart or a dashboard is still a design decision, not just a data one.

This page is where I'll be collecting that visual work over time, from branding and social content to presentation design, alongside two other things I love making: photography and poetry.
