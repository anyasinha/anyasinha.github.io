Language & Culture

Language has always felt like a doorway to me, a way of understanding how other people see the world, not just a subject to study.

That fascination is a big part of why I became a Duolingo English Test Ambassador. Creating promotional content for the platform let me connect with a community of over 40K followers from every corner of the globe, and cheering people on as they cross communication milestones, whether that's passing an exam or finally feeling confident in a second language, never gets old for me.

I'm drawn to the small cultural details that live inside a language: the idioms that don't translate cleanly, the humor that shifts from one country to the next, the way politeness is expressed differently depending on where you are. Exploring that has made me a lot more curious and a lot more patient when navigating unfamiliar contexts, both online and off.

It's also quietly shaped how I communicate professionally. Whether I'm writing marketing copy or explaining a data model, I try to remember that clarity looks different for different audiences, and that's a lesson I first picked up from studying language itself.

  • Cross-cultural curiosityAn ongoing interest in idiom, humor, and communication styles across cultures.
  • Clear communicationApplying language sensitivity to how I write and present, no matter the audience.
